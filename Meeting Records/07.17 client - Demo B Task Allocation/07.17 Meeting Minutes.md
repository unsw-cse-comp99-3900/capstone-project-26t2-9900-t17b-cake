## Decisions

1. The reproduced figures will be revised according to the client’s visualisation requirements.

2. Marker size will remain consistent, while different recordings or dates will be distinguished using different marker shapes.

3. The team will prioritise onset-compensation candidates of 50, 100 and 150 milliseconds.

4. Accuracy, spread and empirical 95% coverage will be analysed and reported separately.

5. Separate full-size ellipse figures will be generated for Calibration, 10-minute and 60-minute stages.

6. The main CSV file will include target position, ellipse centre and spread information.

7. When delay candidates perform similarly, the shortest-equivalent rule will be used.
