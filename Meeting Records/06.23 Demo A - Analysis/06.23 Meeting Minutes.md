## Restriction for Demo A

The team agreed that every Demo A task should:

1. Have one clear objective and expected outcome.
2. Be small enough to be completed and reviewed independently.
3. Have a clearly identified owner.
4. Include measurable completion or acceptance criteria.
5. Include a priority, due date and current status in Jira.
6. Be linked to a relevant user story, system requirement or Demo A objective.
7. Produce verifiable evidence, such as code, a pull request, test results, documentation, diagrams or screenshots.

## Decisions

1. The team will use the Demo A requirements as the basis for determining the next stage of work.

2. All members must use a consistent interpretation of the project scope, features and expected Demo A outcomes.

3. Large or unclear Jira items will be divided into smaller tasks with clear outputs and acceptance criteria.

4. Every Demo A deliverable must have an owner, deadline and progress status.

5. The team will check that the user stories, system design, implementation tasks and Demo A presentation are consistent with one another.

6. The team will conduct an internal rehearsal and review before presenting Demo A.
